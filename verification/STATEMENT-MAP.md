# Original claim and exact Lean coverage

**Object.** In Alexandersson's ordered set-partition question an ordered partition is a list of nonempty disjoint subsets with union \([n]\). A surjection from Lean's zero-based \(\mathrm{Fin}\,n\) onto \(\mathrm{Fin}\,b\) represents the same object: its fiber at position \(i\) is the \(i\)-th block. The labels of the underlying set are shifted by one, without changing any block size. In FirstJump.lean, Layer imposes surjectivity and counts fibers of size at least \(j\); count sums the corresponding cardinalities over \(0\le b\le n\). FirstJumpRows.lean defines row over the reals, and coeff_row identifies each coefficient for a positive threshold. Here \(j=2\), so all singleton blocks remain admissible with weight 1. See [the ordered entry immediately below the unordered entry](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#set-partitions-and-trees).

**Root statement.** OrderedPartitions.original_threshold_two_window takes every natural \(n\) and either \(g=1\) or \(g=2\). Its conclusion RootTracking.OrderedPair uses \(d=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), the old polynomial row n 2 and the new polynomial row (n+g) 2. InterlacingStep.RootArray records the exact degree, a nonzero polynomial and that its strictly increasing list of \(d\) negative real numbers consists of roots. RootArray.exact and root multiplicity one then follow. For equal degree, RightInterlaces places each new root strictly to the right of the corresponding old root and before every subsequent old root. With degree \(d+1\), Interlaces places the old roots strictly between consecutive new roots. The leading and constant coefficients are positive. Rows 0 and 1 have empty lists. Theorem threshold_two_window_sharp uses the concrete pair \(3,6\) whose degrees are \(1,3\).

**Analytic branch.** LastBlockRow and OriginalEGF derive the generating function from the genuine ordered rows. At \(t=-q/(1-q)\), it is \((1-q)/G_q(z)\) with \(G_q(z)=1-2q-z+qe^z\). The Taylor coefficient of \(1/G_q\) is therefore \(O_{n,2}(t)/((1-q)n!)\). TwoPoleOriginalTaylor.lean proves this identity from the original counts. TwoPoleResidues.lean proves the exact complex contour identity on the oscillatory interval. TwoPolePhaseEndpoints.lean proves continuity and the limiting phase values \(\pi/2\) and \((n+2)\pi/2\). The phase readout uses positive \(\sin(g\theta)\) and does not assume monotonicity of the phase. The estimates of TwoPoleTwoSteps.lean apply starting at old index \(20\) for \(g=1\) and \(25\) for \(g=2\).

**Finite branch.** TwoPoleFiniteVerified.lean independently supplies the original row order for old sizes \(2,\ldots,21\) at distance one (20 pairs) and \(2,\ldots,27\) at distance two (26 pairs). The finite values overlap both analytic branches. TwoPoleFirstRows.lean covers all four pairs with old size 0 or 1. TwoPoleAllSizes.lean combines both branches without any pole, error, positivity, certificate or nondegeneracy assumptions in the final root theorem.

**Relationship to the broader conjecture.** This is the full fixed-threshold-two result. It also settles the pair of rows two ranks apart for that threshold. The all-threshold ordered problem, the unordered problem, any wider window at other thresholds, and any rightmost-root asymptotic are outside the formal entry. In particular the sharpness theorem concerns the length of a pairwise-interlacing window and does not assert a limiting root location.
